# PWX-0010: Remove period and comma from the symbol set

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Supersedes:** PWX-0009

## Context
While testing the popup, the period (`.`) and comma (`,`) proved hard to tell
apart and easy to miss when reading a generated password, especially next to
each other or at the end of the password.

## Options considered
1. **Keep the PWX-0009 set** (26 symbols), including `.` and `,`.
2. **Remove `.` and `,`** (24 symbols).
3. **Build the "exclude ambiguous characters" toggle now**, covering these
   along with `0/O` and `1/l/I`.

## Decision
Remove `.` and `,` (option 2). The symbol set is now: `!@#$%^&*()_+-=[]{}|;:<>?`

The broader ambiguous-characters toggle (PWX-0007) stays deferred to a later
version.

## Consequences
- Passwords are easier to read when they have to be typed by hand.
- The full pool shrinks from 88 to 86 characters. Entropy at length 16 drops
  from about 103.4 to about 102.8 bits, and at 32 from about 206.7 to about
  205.6 bits: a negligible change.
- The entropy tests (5.1 and 5.2) are updated to the new values.
