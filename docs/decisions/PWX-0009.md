# PWX-0009: Use an expanded symbol set

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-18
**Supersedes:** PWX-0004

## Context
PWX-0004 chose a 12-character safe subset of symbols (`!@#$%^&*-_=+`) to
minimize rejections by websites. During implementation, a larger set was
preferred for stronger passwords at the same length.

## Options considered
1. **Keep the safe subset** from PWX-0004 (12 symbols).
2. **Expanded set:** `!@#$%^&*()_+-=[]{}|;:,.<>?` (26 symbols).
3. **All printable ASCII symbols**, including quotes, backslash, backtick,
   tilde, slash, and space.

## Decision
Use the expanded set (option 2): `!@#$%^&*()_+-=[]{}|;:,.<>?`

Quotes, backslash, backtick, tilde, slash, and space remain excluded, since
they are the characters most likely to be rejected or mishandled by websites.

## Consequences
- The full pool grows from 74 to 88 characters. Entropy at the default
  length of 16 rises from about 99 to about 103 bits, and at 32 from about
  199 to about 207 bits.
- Some sites may reject characters in the new set, such as `<`, `>`, `;`,
  or `|`. The user can regenerate, or turn off symbols for those sites.
- The entropy figures quoted in PWX-0005 and PWX-0007 assumed the 74-character
  pool. They remain correct as lower bounds; the current values are in
  `docs/design/generator-spec.md`.
- A user-editable symbol list is still a possible later improvement, through
  its own ADR.
